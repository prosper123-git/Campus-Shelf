About CampusShelf

CampusShelf is a digital textbook platform built for University students. Every semester, students spend a significant amount on physical textbooks that are expensive to print and heavy to carry across a campus spread over a hilltop. CampusShelf solves this by giving lecturers a way to publish their course textbooks digitally, and students a way to get instant, affordable access — right from their phone or laptop.

Because every textbook used at the University is written by lecturer, CampusShelf works directly with course authors and departments to bring official course material online, rather than reselling outside publishers' content.

What we're trying to do

  • Lower the cost of course materials. No title on CampusShelf is priced above ₦7,000, well under typical print costs.
  • Keep it official. Every book is linked to a real course code and uploaded directly by the lecturer or department that wrote it.
  • Make studying lighter, literally. Read your books in-app, on any device, without carrying physical copies between hostels and lecture halls.
  • Respect how the University works. CampusShelf is a platform for lecturers and departments to distribute their own material to students — it is not a marketplace for students to buy or sell among themselves.

Who it's for

CampusShelf is built for University undergraduate and postgraduate students across all colleges — from Basic and Applied Sciences to Business, Law, Theology and Nursing — and for the lecturers who write the textbooks they study from.
